Maintenance Tips

Luggage Care Guide

Safe, practical recommendations to maintain the longevity of your travel suitcases.

Avoid Unnecessary Overloading

Packing beyond full capacity strains zippers and outer shell seams during handling.

Keep Clean and Dry

Wipe exterior surfaces with a soft damp cloth and ensure full drying before storage.

Handle Wheels Carefully

Avoid dragging wheels forcibly over steep drop-offs or unusually jagged gravel paths.

Proper Off-Season Storage

Store suitcases in dry areas away from direct prolonged humidity when not in active use.

Smooth Surface Rolling

Lift luggage slightly over curbs to reduce direct heavy structural shock on wheel mounts.

Pre-Travel Checkup

Regularly inspect telescopic handles and zipper tracks prior to heading out on trips.
